Local network inventory constraints

2026-11-01

Reachability helpers default to private and loopback ranges. Public CIDRs are rejected unless policy is explicitly relaxed. That is a product decision for defensive use: accidental internet-wide probing should be hard. ICMP reachability also depends on host permissions — failures are reported as failures, not painted as empty networks.
